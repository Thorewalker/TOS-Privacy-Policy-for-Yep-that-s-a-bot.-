        <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

        <p class="updated">
            Last updated: August 14, 2026
        </p>

        <p>
            This Privacy Policy explains how
            <strong>Yep, that's a bot.</strong>
            ("the Bot") accesses, uses, stores, and protects information
            when operating through Discord.
        </p>

        <p>
            The Bot is intended to provide utility, role management,
            music playback, server tools, and other features to Discord
            communities in which it has been authorized to operate.
        </p>

        <div class="notice">
            The Bot is designed to collect and permanently store as little
            personal information as reasonably necessary to provide its
            functionality.
        </div>

        <h2>1. Information the Bot May Access</h2>

        <p>
            While operating, the Bot may receive information made available
            through Discord's API, including:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li>Discord user IDs</li>
            <li>Discord server (guild) IDs</li>
            <li>Role IDs</li>
            <li>Channel IDs</li>
            <li>Usernames or display names when necessary for a feature</li>
            <li>Server configuration information</li>
            <li>Slash command interactions and command parameters</li>
            <li>Information necessary to perform requested role-management functions</li>
            <li>Voice channel information required to provide music playback</li>
        </ul>

        <p>
            The Bot may temporarily process additional Discord information
            when necessary to complete a requested command or feature.
        </p>

        <h2>2. Information the Bot May Store</h2>

        <p>
            The Bot may store limited information required for configuration
            or continued operation, such as:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li>Server IDs</li>
            <li>Role IDs used for automatic or self-assignable roles</li>
            <li>Channel IDs used for configured bot functions</li>
            <li>Server-specific Bot settings</li>
            <li>User IDs when necessary for a persistent feature</li>
            <li>Administrative configuration information</li>
        </ul>

        <p>
            Temporary information, such as music queues, command state,
            and active voice-session information, may be held in memory
            while a feature is in use and discarded when it is no longer
            required.
        </p>

        <h2>3. Message Content</h2>

        <p>
            The Bot is not intended to permanently collect or archive normal
            Discord conversations.
        </p>

        <p>
            Message content may be temporarily accessed if a specific Bot
            feature requires it and the Bot has been granted the necessary
            Discord permissions. Unless explicitly required by a feature,
            message content is not permanently stored.
        </p>

        <p>
            Message content obtained through Discord will not be used to
            train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
        </p>

        <h2>4. Voice and Audio</h2>

        <p>
            The Bot may connect to Discord voice channels in order to provide
            music or other audio-related functionality.
        </p>

        <p>
            The Bot does not record, permanently store, or create recordings
            of users' voice communications.
        </p>

        <p>
            Voice-channel membership and connection information may be
            temporarily processed as necessary for the Bot to connect to,
            remain in, or disconnect from a voice channel.
        </p>

        <h2>5. Music Searches and External Services</h2>

        <p>
            When users request music or other externally sourced content,
            search terms, URLs, or similar request information may be sent
            to third-party services that are necessary to locate or deliver
            the requested content.
        </p>

        <p>
            Those third-party services operate according to their own terms
            and privacy policies.
        </p>

        <p>
            The Bot does not sell music-search information or use it for
            advertising.
        </p>

        <h2>6. Server Integrations</h2>

        <p>
            The Bot may provide integrations with privately operated game
            servers or other services.
        </p>

        <p>
            These integrations may process information such as server status,
            player counts, player names, uptime information, or administrative
            commands when necessary to provide the requested integration.
        </p>

        <p>
            Information from these integrations will only be used for the
            operation of the relevant feature.
        </p>

        <h2>7. Logging</h2>

        <p>
            The Bot may maintain limited technical logs for troubleshooting,
            security, and reliability purposes.
        </p>

        <p>
            Logs may include information such as:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li>Error messages</li>
            <li>Command failures</li>
            <li>Server IDs</li>
            <li>User IDs associated with failed or administrative commands</li>
            <li>Timestamps</li>
            <li>Technical diagnostic information</li>
        </ul>

        <p>
            Logging is intended for maintenance and security and is not used
            to profile users.
        </p>

        <h2>8. How Information Is Used</h2>

        <p>
            Information accessed or stored by the Bot is used only when
            reasonably necessary to:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li>Provide requested Bot functionality</li>
            <li>Manage configured roles</li>
            <li>Respond to commands</li>
            <li>Provide music or voice-related functionality</li>
            <li>Provide server integrations</li>
            <li>Store server-specific configuration</li>
            <li>Diagnose errors and maintain reliability</li>
            <li>Prevent abuse or unauthorized administrative actions</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>9. Sale and Advertising</h2>

        <p>
            Information obtained through Discord is
            <strong>not sold, rented, licensed, or provided to advertising
            networks or data brokers.</strong>
        </p>

        <p>
            The Bot does not use Discord user information for targeted
            advertising.
        </p>

        <h2>10. Sharing of Information</h2>

        <p>
            Information is not intentionally shared with unrelated third
            parties except when:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li>
                A third-party service is necessary to provide a feature
                specifically requested by a user;
            </li>

            <li>
                A hosting or infrastructure provider processes information
                as necessary to operate the Bot;
            </li>

            <li>
                Disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal
                process;
            </li>

            <li>
                Disclosure is reasonably necessary to investigate abuse,
                security incidents, or threats to the Bot or its users.
            </li>
        </ul>

        <h2>11. Data Retention</h2>

        <p>
            Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary
            to provide the feature for which it was collected, maintain Bot
            configuration, troubleshoot technical issues, meet legal
            obligations, or protect the security of the Bot.
        </p>

        <p>
            Temporary information may be deleted automatically when a session,
            command, music queue, or other temporary feature ends.
        </p>

        <p>
            Server-specific stored information may be deleted when the Bot is
            permanently removed from a server, except where limited information
            must temporarily be retained for security, backup, or legal
            purposes.
        </p>

        <h2>12. Data Security</h2>

        <p>
            Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to
            protect stored information from unauthorized access, disclosure,
            alteration, or destruction.
        </p>

        <p>
            However, no computer system or internet-connected service can be
            guaranteed to be completely secure.
        </p>
